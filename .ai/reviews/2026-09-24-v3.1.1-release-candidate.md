## Browser engines

Playwright's official Firefox 153.0 and WebKit 26.5 runtimes executed both
regular and minified browser artifacts. Each combination passed:

- standalone `OrbCompatibility.checkCompatibility()` before loading orb.js;
- `Orb.checkCompatibility()` after loading;
- legacy Moon coordinates and observer azimuth/elevation;
- Alpha-5 catalogue decoding;
- a browser-bundled structured `AstroInstant`, EPV00 Sun, and observer
  calculation with metadata.

All four runs returned the same numerical values and no page errors. This
closes the recorded Firefox/WebKit execution gap. It does not claim testing on
every historical browser version in the declared support range.
